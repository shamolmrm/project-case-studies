POST/users/auth/logout/Blacklist refresh tokenJWT
GET/users/me/Get current authenticated user profileJWT
Products
GET/products/List products with filter, search, sort, paginationPublic
POST/products/Create new productAdmin
GET/products/{slug}/Product detail by slugPublic

POST/products/bulk-upload/Import products from CSV fileAdmin
GET/products/export/Export all products as CSVAdmin
Orders

GET/orders/{id}/Order detail (accepts ID or order_number)JWT
PATCH/orders/{id}/update-status/Update order status (pending→shipped etc.)Admin
GET/orders/{id}/invoice/Download PDF invoice for orderJWT
Cart & Payments

POST/cart/add/Add product to cartJWT

POST/payments/stripe/webhook/Stripe webhook for payment confirmationPublic

Key Features

🔐 JWT Auth
Access + Refresh tokens, role-based access (superadmin, manager, staff, customer)
🛒 Shopping Cart
Redux-persisted cart, coupon validation, quantity management
💳 Payments
Stripe Payment Intents + PayPal integration with webhooks
📦 Inventory
Multi-warehouse stock tracking, low-stock alerts, adjustments
📊 Analytics
Revenue charts, top products, customer reports, CSV/Excel export
🏷️ Mega Menu
2-column category dropdown with parent/child hierarchy navigation
⚡ Flash Sale
Auto countdown timer, Swiper carousel, sale_price filter
📄 PDF Invoice
ReportLab-generated order invoices downloadable from admin
